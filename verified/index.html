Every site, note, distance and score in Site-Drill traces back to a published source or a published standard. This page lists them. Found a mistake? Report a site or map error.

Each site has at least two sources, and every name and number in its hint, note and key points must appear in that site's source notes (checked automatically). Notes are original writing; they never copy a source or Wikipedia. 23 sites were also checked by hand, including every site whose identification or interpretation is debated. Positions come from Wikidata, corrected from published excavation reports or ASI and UNESCO records where those disagree (34 sites).

The map

The outline of India comes from Natural Earth (1:10m, India point of view) and was compared line by line with the Survey of India's 1:16 million political map: median difference 2 km, with all of Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Arunachal Pradesh, Lakshadweep and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands present. It shows no state borders and no historical borders.

Distances and scores

  • Distances: great-circle distance on a sphere of radius 6371.0088 km, tested against Karney (2013) geodesic reference values to within 0.5%.
  • Map projection: Lambert conformal conic with India's national parameters, tested against Snyder (1987) and EPSG:7755.
  • Scores: within 25 km = 10, 50 km = 8, 100 km = 6, 200 km = 3, farther = 0; a hint costs 2 points.
  • Review schedule: FSRS-6 (via ts-fsrs), tested against the library's published reference sequences.
